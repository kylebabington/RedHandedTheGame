# Pitch Strategy — The Evidence Room

**Status:** Approved planning guidance from the master specification.  
**Implementation status:** Not started (Milestone 11). No outreach until the demo is pitch-ready.

## Objective

Demonstrate that RedHanded could have a commercially viable, visually distinctive interactive product—not merely that a game can be built.

## Pitch package (planned deliverables)

1. Private playable demo (Case 001)
2. Pitch deck (~10–12 slides)
3. Promotional trailer (60–90 seconds of real gameplay)
4. Product one-pager
5. Commercial proposal (negotiation options only)
6. Technical documentation
7. Outreach email

## Intellectual property

- Prototype is explicitly unofficial and unaffiliated.
- Use original or appropriately licensed assets only.
- No official artwork, logos, host likenesses, or recordings without permission.
- No AI imitation of host voices.
- Official episode trivia reserved for a licensed version.

## Commercial hypotheses (not guarantees)

- Audience engagement via interactive mysteries
- Premium / membership-linked case releases (e.g. Patreon-aligned)
- Brand expansion into interactive entertainment

## Quality bar before outreach

First-time players can finish without coaching; no unwinnable states; mobile-complete without drag-only interactions; mute/captions/reduced-motion; critical routes tested; unofficial labeling intact; implemented vs proposed features clearly distinguished.
